🌱 Getting started

What is VRIKHSHAM?

VRIKHSHAM is an intelligent plant care platform that helps you organize your garden, learn about plant care, track health records, and receive practical reminders.

Do I need an account to use VRIKHSHAM?

You can explore public sections such as the homepage and plant care guidance without an account. Creating a free account is required to save a garden, add plants, and view personal reminders.

How do I add my first plant?

Register or log in, open My Garden, and use the plant form to add a name and the basic details of your plant. You can then manage it from your garden dashboard.

🔬 Features

How does the AI Plant Doctor work?

The Plant Doctor is designed to guide you through plant health concerns. Share the relevant plant symptoms or details, then use the suggested observations and care actions as a starting point for diagnosis.

Can VRIKHSHAM tell me when to water my plants?

Yes. VRIKHSHAM supports care reminders and plant care guidance. Reminders can help you plan watering and maintenance based on the schedule you add for each plant.

Where can I view a plant's health history?

Open Health History from the navigation menu. It brings together saved plant health information and previous records for plants in your account.

Is the care advice suitable for every plant?

General guidance is a useful foundation, but plant needs vary by species, climate, soil, and season. When in doubt, use species-specific advice or consult a local horticulture expert.

🔒 Account & privacy

How is my account information protected?

Your account uses authentication to restrict access to personal garden features. Use a strong, unique password and avoid sharing your login details with others.

Can I remove my plants or garden data?

Garden management features allow you to maintain the plants and records associated with your account. If you need assistance removing information that cannot be edited, contact the project owner.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery option available on the login page, when enabled for your deployment. If it is unavailable, contact the site administrator for help restoring access.
